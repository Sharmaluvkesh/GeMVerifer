## 🎬 Master Timed Scene Breakdown & Voiceover Narration Script

```carousel
### ⏱️ SCENE 1: THE PROBLEM (0:00 - 0:15)
**Visual**: Clean title card "GeMVerifier V2 — AI-assisted Bid Verification & Evidence Platform". Stack of document cards appears (Tender Document, Financial Statement, GST, PAN, Experience Certs). Manual cross-checking complexity animation.
**Voiceover**: "Government procurement involves checking multiple eligibility, statutory and tender-specific requirements across large numbers of bidder documents. Manual examination and cross-checking can make bid verification document-intensive and time-consuming."
**Lower-Third**: ⚠️ Challenge: Manual document-intensive cross-checking across multi-page proposals.
<!-- slide -->
### ⏱️ SCENE 2: INTRODUCE OUR SOLUTION (0:15 - 0:30)
**Visual**: Transition into GeMVerifier V2 application dashboard. 4 core pillars highlighted: 1. AI Understands, 2. Evidence Proves, 3. Engine Validates, 4. Officer Decides.
**Voiceover**: "GeMVerifier V2 is an AI-assisted bid verification and evidence platform designed to structure this process around traceable evidence and deterministic verification."
**Lower-Third**: 🛡️ GeMVerifier V2 • Structuring procurement around traceable evidence.
<!-- slide -->
### ⏱️ SCENE 3: SYSTEM WORKFLOW (0:30 - 0:45)
**Visual**: Animated 8-stage pipeline flowchart: Tender → Requirements → Bidder Documents → AI Extraction → Evidence Mapping → Verification Engine → PASS / FAIL / REVIEW → Officer Decision.
**Voiceover**: "Our workflow begins with the tender and its requirements. The system analyzes requirements, processes bidder documents, extracts relevant information, maps evidence back to its source, and then applies deterministic verification rules. The officer reviews the evidence and makes the final decision."
**Lower-Third**: ⚡ End-to-End Traceable Bid Verification Workflow
<!-- slide -->
### ⏱️ SCENE 4: TENDER REQUIREMENTS (0:45 - 1:00)
**Visual**: Prototype tender screen (Ref: GEM/2026/B/99012). Highlighting text "Minimum Turnover: ₹2 Crore" → Structuring into task: Parameter: Turnover, Operator: >=, Threshold: ₹2 Crore, Mandatory: Yes.
**Voiceover**: "First, the system converts natural-language tender conditions into structured verification tasks. For example, a requirement for minimum turnover of two crore rupees becomes a measurable rule: turnover greater than or equal to two crore."
**Lower-Third**: 📐 Natural Language Tender Conditions ➔ Structured Verification Rules
<!-- slide -->
### ⏱️ SCENE 5: BIDDER DOCUMENT UPLOAD (1:00 - 1:20)
**Visual**: Vendor Portal uploading proposal files for fictional bidder "ABC Technologies Pvt. Ltd.": FinancialStatement.pdf, GST_Certificate.pdf, PAN_Card.pdf, Experience_Certificate.pdf. Processing animation: PDF → OCR/Parser → AI Document Understanding.
**Voiceover**: "Next, the bidder submits the required documents. Our document pipeline can process digital PDFs and scanned documents using parsing and OCR. AI is then used for document understanding, classification and structured field extraction."
**Lower-Third**: 📄 PDF Parsing + Tesseract OCR + AI Field Understanding Pipeline
<!-- slide -->
### ⏱️ SCENE 6: AI EXTRACTION (1:20 - 1:35)
**Visual**: Financial statement document view. Highlighted turnover value ₹2.43 Crore. Side metadata panel: Document Type: Financial Statement, Field: Turnover, Value: ₹2.43 Crore, Source: FinancialStatement.pdf, Page: 7.
**Voiceover**: "For example, the system can identify a financial statement, extract the relevant turnover value of two point four three crore rupees, and identify where the evidence appears in the document."
**Lower-Third**: 🔍 AI Field Extraction with Source Page Attribution
<!-- slide -->
### ⏱️ SCENE 7: EVIDENCE MAPPING (1:35 - 1:55)
**Visual**: Interactive Evidence Chain Graph: Requirement (Min Turnover ≥ ₹2 Cr) → Source Doc (FinancialStatement.pdf) → Page (Page 7) → Extracted Value (₹2.43 Cr) → Verification Rule (2.43 ≥ 2) → Result (PASS ✓).
**Voiceover**: "This is a key part of GeMVerifier V2: evidence mapping. Instead of simply producing a compliance result, the system maintains a traceable chain from the requirement to the original document, page, extracted value and verification rule."
**Lower-Third**: 🔗 Traceable Chain: Requirement ➔ Document ➔ Page ➔ Extracted Value ➔ Rule
<!-- slide -->
### ⏱️ SCENE 8: DETERMINISTIC VERIFICATION (1:55 - 2:10)
**Visual**: Deterministic Verification Engine view. Displaying Required: ₹2.00 Cr, Bidder: ₹2.43 Cr. Evaluating rule: 2.43 ≥ 2.0 → TRUE ✓ → Result: PASS ✓. Highlight banner: "AI extracts evidence | Backend validates the rule".
**Voiceover**: "The actual compliance decision is performed by the deterministic verification engine. Here, the requirement is two crore rupees and the extracted value is two point four three crore. The backend evaluates two point four three greater than or equal to two, producing PASS."
**Lower-Third**: ⚙️ Deterministic Backend Validation (AI extracts proof, backend runs math)
<!-- slide -->
### ⏱️ SCENE 9: PASS / FAIL / REVIEW OUTCOMES (2:10 - 2:30)
**Visual**: Officer Dashboard listing requirements for ABC Technologies Pvt. Ltd.:
✓ Minimum Turnover — PASS
✓ PAN Verification — PASS
✕ Experience Requirement (Req: 5 Yrs • Offered: 3 Yrs) — FAIL
⚠ GST Evidence Expiry — REVIEW
**Voiceover**: "Our system produces three explainable outcomes. PASS when a requirement is clearly satisfied. FAIL when the evidence clearly violates the requirement. And REVIEW when evidence is missing, ambiguous, conflicting or requires human verification."
**Lower-Third**: 📊 Explainable Outcomes: PASS ✓ | FAIL ✕ | REVIEW ⚠
<!-- slide -->
### ⏱️ SCENE 10: EVIDENCE-BASED OFFICER REVIEW (2:30 - 2:45)
**Visual**: Clicking on REVIEW item (GST Exemption Expiry). Opening inspection drawer showing full audit trail: Requirement → Source Document (GST_Certificate.pdf, Page 2) → Extracted Information → Rule → Result.
**Voiceover**: "The officer can inspect why a requirement passed, failed or requires review, and directly trace the result back to its supporting evidence. This makes the verification process explainable and auditable."
**Lower-Third**: 🔎 Deep Evidence Inspection & Complete Auditability
<!-- slide -->
### ⏱️ SCENE 11: HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP CONTROL (2:45 - 2:55)
**Visual**: Workflow diagram highlighting OFFICER as the final stage: AI → Evidence → Verification → Officer → Final Decision. Highlight badge: "Officer Retains 100% Final Authority".
**Voiceover**: "Most importantly, AI does not make the final procurement decision. The system provides evidence and verification support, while the final qualification or disqualification decision remains with the procurement officer."
**Lower-Third**: 👤 Human-in-the-Loop: Officer retains final decision authority.
<!-- slide -->
### ⏱️ SCENE 12: FINAL CLOSING (2:55 - 3:05)
**Visual**: Return to GeMVerifier V2 logo & motto:
"AI understands. Evidence proves. The verification engine validates. The officer decides."
Clean professional fade-out.
**Voiceover**: "GeMVerifier V2 transforms bid verification into an evidence-driven workflow. AI understands. Evidence proves. The verification engine validates. The officer decides."
**Lower-Third**: 🎓 GeMVerifier V2 — Smart India Hackathon (SIH 2026) Project Presentation
```
